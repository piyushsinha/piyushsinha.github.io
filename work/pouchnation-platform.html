One product strategy, many markets.

Role: Director of Digital Experience  ·  Years: 2025 – present  ·  Scope: Distributed, multi-market org  ·  Domain: Product & platform strategy

Context

PouchNATION is a platform business building for markets across Southeast Asia, South Asia, MENA, South America, and the EU. My mandate is to own product and platform strategy across that footprint.

The challenge

A distributed platform business doesn't usually fail on product quality — it fails because no one owns the whole picture, and every market drifts toward its own version of the truth. The job is to run one roadmap instead of five.

What I did

I own platform and payments product strategy across the target markets, driving alignment across Product, Engineering, Operations, and Sales toward a single source of truth. I manage the portfolio across MVP, scale, and enterprise tiers — sequencing the roadmap against commercial priorities so teams work on the highest-impact thing at each stage. And I designed and run a structured AI evaluation programme across six workflow categories, assessing tools against a productivity baseline, quality delta, cost model, and go/no-go with ROI — then rolling the selected tools out to the team with best practices.
