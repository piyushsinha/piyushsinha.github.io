Role: Founder, aamzng — research lead  ·  Client: TTB (post TMB + Thanachart merger)  ·  Scope: Eight business units  ·  Domain: Retail banking

Context

When TMB and Thanachart merged to form TTB, the combined bank needed a single retail banking app — TTB Touch — that could serve two customer bases and eight business units without fracturing the experience.

The challenge

A merger is the hardest possible context for product direction: two organisations, two sets of customers, two ways of working, and eight business units each with a claim on the app. Getting to one coherent product meant getting to one evidence base first.
