Community

UX Happy Hour Bangkok

Founder, UX Happy Hour Bangkok
Co-Organizer, UX Happy Hour Chicago

UX Happy Hour is a monthly meetup of UX professionals in multiple cities around the world. I used to co-organize the Chicago chapter alongside Shay Howe between Jan 2012 and Jan 2014.

After I moved to Bangkok, I launched UX Happy Hour Bangkok in 2015 and grew the meetup to a community of over 1,700 people. UX Happy Hour Bangkok came to a finale in 2025 after 10 years of successful events.
